What is this site for?

A web application designed to help users visually and auditorily understand how algorithms work.

Instead of just reading code, users can experience the world of sorting (like Bubble Sort and Quick Sort) with their eyes and ears.

Design Adopted: Core Aesthetic

  • Modern & Premium: Utilizes a dark theme with glassmorphism (frosted glass) effects on control panels.
  • Typography: Clean, modern 'Inter' font. Gradients applied to typography for a vibrant, high-tech feel.
  • Colors: Deep background contrasting with bright neon accents (cyan, blue, purple) to draw focus to interactive elements.

Design Adopted: UX & Visualization

  • Auditory Feedback: Emits electronic sounds corresponding to data values during swapping, providing a multi-sensory experience.
  • Rainbow Gradient Data: Array bars are colored with a value-based rainbow gradient, forming a beautiful spectrum as sorting completes.
  • Interactive Highlighting: Clear visual cues during algorithm execution (Yellow for comparing, Red for swapping).
  • Rewarding Completion: A satisfying sweep animation light-up sequence plays upon sorting completion.

Summary

The project goes beyond a simple tool. It combines modern web aesthetics, rich interactivity, and multi-sensory feedback to turn a complex computer science subject into an engaging, premium experience.
